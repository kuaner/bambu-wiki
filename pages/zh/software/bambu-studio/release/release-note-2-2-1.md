V2.2.1是基于V2.2.0的一个版本，新增支持[Bambu Lab H2S](https://bambulab.com/en)，并修复了部分已知错误。

![1280x1280.png](https://wiki.bambulab.com/bambu-studio/1280x1280.png)

### Bug修复

1. 修复了多设备功能打开时校准选项卡消失的错误。([#7697](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/7697))
2. 修复了动态流量校准时步长无法设置为0.001的错误。([#7882](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/7882), [#7850](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/7850))
3. 修复导入使用第三方打印机的3mf文件时可能导致软件崩溃的问题。([#7639](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/7639))
4. 修复了部分用户侧边栏无法拖动的问题。([#7939](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/7939))
5. 修复了P系列、A系列部分情况下外挂进料按钮无法点击的问题。([#7888](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/7888))
6. 修复了调色盘自定义颜色无法保存的问题。([#7854](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/7854), [#5441](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/5441))
7. 修复了自动朝向功能在命令行中行为与GUI不一致的问题。([#6092](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/6092))
8. 修复了因新文字工具导致的部分居中与文字间隔错误。([#7842](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/7842), [#7835](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/7835), [#7893](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/7893), [#7828](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/issues/7828))
9. 给H2D机型新增Polymaker Fiberon系列耗材的预设，感谢[@Pepe-Polymaker](https://github.com/Pepe-Polymaker)的贡献。
