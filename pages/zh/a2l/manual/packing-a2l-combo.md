## 装箱提示

规范打包是避免运输损伤最有效的方式，能最大程度地保障货物安全、减少不必要的经济损失。

> **为确保您的设备安全抵达目的地，我们特别提醒您:**
>
> - **请检查所有螺丝是否拧紧**，防止元件松动。
> - **请确认缓冲泡棉是否放置到位**，充分保护设备。

> 请将拆下的螺丝和配件妥善放回包装内，避免运输过程中与其他部件发生碰撞，导致外观或结构受损，影响您的退换货权益。

## 准备工具

- H2.0 内六角扳手
- Z 轴固定器螺丝，规格：M3×10
- 镊子
- 透明胶
- A5 纸，尺寸：148 x 210 mm
- 扎带，用于固定 AMS lite 主体，尺寸：长 495 mm，宽 5 mm
- 较宽扎带，用于固定底座泡棉，尺寸：长 1200 mm，宽 8.8 mm

> 如果没有 1.2 m 长的扎带，可将多个较宽的短扎带连接使用，确保总长度足够固定底座泡棉。

## 操作步骤

### 1. 关闭电源，收起线缆

- 关闭打印机电源，拔出电源线。  
  ![a2l-pack-001-s01-poweroff-unplug-power-cord.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-001-s01-poweroff-unplug-power-cord.png)
- 按紧线缆接口顶端，断开 AMS lite 的 4-pin 连接线。  
  ![a2l-pack-002-s01-disconnect-ams-4pin-cable.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-002-s01-disconnect-ams-4pin-cable.png)
- 安装好电源插头保护套，卷起电源线并用皮筋固定。  
  ![a2l-pack-004-s01-install-power-plug-protector.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-004-s01-install-power-plug-protector.png)
- 盖上摄像头隐私盖。  
  ![a2l-pack-005-s01-close-camera-privacy-cover.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-005-s01-close-camera-privacy-cover.png)

### 2. 拆除铁氟龙料管

- 拆除料管中继接头。  
  ![a2l-pack-006-s02-remove-ptfe-relay-connector.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-006-s02-remove-ptfe-relay-connector.png)

> 推荐使用镊子进行辅助。

- 按压按压工具头进料口黑色外圈，拔出四根料管。  
  ![a2l-pack-007-s02-press-toolhead-inlet-collar.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-007-s02-press-toolhead-inlet-collar.png)
- 按压料盘支架上的料管接口黑色外圈，拆除料管。  
  ![a2l-pack-009-s02-remove-spool-holder-ptfe-tube.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-009-s02-remove-spool-holder-ptfe-tube.png)

### 3. 用胶带固定部件

- 用胶带固定工具头五通组件。  
  ![a2l-pack-010-s03-tape-five-way-connector.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-010-s03-tape-five-way-connector.png)
- 用胶带固定 X 电机盒。  
  ![a2l-pack-011-s03-tape-x-motor-box-front.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-011-s03-tape-x-motor-box-front.png)

### 4. 拆除 AMS lite

- 握住卡爪，依次拔出所有 AMS lite 料盘转轴。  
  ![a2l-pack-013-s04-remove-ams-lite-spool-axle-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-013-s04-remove-ams-lite-spool-axle-1.png)
- 拆除连接 AMS lite 主体和底座支架的四颗螺丝。  
  ![a2l-pack-015-s04-remove-ams-lite-base-screws-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-015-s04-remove-ams-lite-base-screws-1.png)
- 拆分 AMS lite 主体和支架。  
  ![a2l-pack-017-s04-separate-ams-lite-body-base.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-017-s04-separate-ams-lite-body-base.png)
- 用绑带收好线束，并将线束穿过轴。  
  ![a2l-pack-018-s04-tie-cable-bundle-through-axle.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-018-s04-tie-cable-bundle-through-axle.png)
- 用胶带固定 AMS lite 上下料组件上盖。  
  ![a2l-pack-019-s04-tape-ams-lite-feeder-cover.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-019-s04-tape-ams-lite-feeder-cover.png)
- 用扎带固定住 AMS lite 主体以及上下料组件，剪掉多余扎带。  
  ![a2l-pack-020-s04-zip-tie-ams-lite-body.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-020-s04-zip-tie-ams-lite-body.png)  
  ![a2l-pack-022-s04-cut-extra-zip-tie.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-022-s04-cut-extra-zip-tie.png)

### 5. 拆除料盘支架

- 从龙门架上取下料盘支架。  
  ![a2l-pack-023-s05-remove-external-spool-holder-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-023-s05-remove-external-spool-holder-1.png)

### 6. 取下打印板

![a2l-pack-025-s06-remove-print-plate-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-025-s06-remove-print-plate-1.png)

### 7. 折叠触摸屏

![a2l-pack-027-s07-fold-touchscreen-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-027-s07-fold-touchscreen-1.png)

### 8. 拆除吐料组件

- 将工具头移到中间，打开吐料组件后盖。  
  ![a2l-pack-029-s08-cutter-move-toolhead-center-open-cover.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-029-s08-cutter-move-toolhead-center-open-cover.png)
- 打开吐料组件后盖，断开连接线。  
  ![a2l-pack-030-s08-cutter-disconnect-cable-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-030-s08-cutter-disconnect-cable-1.png)
- 移除 1 颗吐料组件螺丝。  
  ![a2l-pack-032-s08-cutter-remove-screw.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-032-s08-cutter-remove-screw.png)
- 用手拿住 X 轴末端的线缆，确保滑槽不被遮挡，滑动取下吐料组件。  
  ![a2l-pack-033-s08-cutter-slide-off-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-033-s08-cutter-slide-off-1.png)
- 盖上吐料组件后盖。  
  ![a2l-pack-035-s08-cutter-close-back-cover.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-035-s08-cutter-close-back-cover.png)
- 整理线缆，并用胶带固定。  
  ![a2l-pack-036-s08-cutter-tape-cable-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-036-s08-cutter-tape-cable-1.png)  
  ![a2l-pack-038-s08-cutter-tape-cable-3.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-038-s08-cutter-tape-cable-3.png)

### 9. 安装 Z 轴固定器

- 将工具头移动至最左侧。  
  ![a2l-pack-040-s09-zlock-move-toolhead-left.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-040-s09-zlock-move-toolhead-left.png)
- 在图示位置安装两个 Z 轴固定器。  
  ![a2l-pack-041-s09-zlock-install-two-locks.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-041-s09-zlock-install-two-locks.png)
- 用 H2.0 扳手拧紧 4 颗螺丝。  
  ![a2l-pack-042-s09-zlock-tighten-4-screws.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-042-s09-zlock-tighten-4-screws.png)

### 10. 移除整合线缆

- 移除 2 颗整合线缆安装螺丝，拔出线缆。  
  ![a2l-pack-043-s10-harness-remove-2-screws.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-043-s10-harness-remove-2-screws.png)

### 11. 断开 Z 电机线

- 将热床推至屏幕端，移除 Z 电机盖，将 Z 电机盖放回配件盒中。  
  ![a2l-pack-045-s11-zmotor-remove-cover.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-045-s11-zmotor-remove-cover.png)
- 拔出 Z 电机线。  
  ![a2l-pack-046-s11-zmotor-disconnect-cable.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-046-s11-zmotor-disconnect-cable.png)

### 12. 解锁底座，拆下龙门架

- 向上抬起 Y 轴上盖的后端，轻轻取出 Y 轴上盖。  
  ![a2l-pack-047-s12-basegantry-remove-y-cover-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-047-s12-basegantry-remove-y-cover-1.png)
- 移除标记位置的 10 颗底座锁定螺丝。  
  ![a2l-pack-049-s12-basegantry-remove-10-base-screws.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-049-s12-basegantry-remove-10-base-screws.png)
- 将热床推至中间。  
  ![a2l-pack-050-s12-basegantry-push-bed-middle.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-050-s12-basegantry-push-bed-middle.png)
- 对齐 Y 轴上盖前端卡扣和安装槽，轻轻将其滑入原位。  
  ![a2l-pack-051-s12-basegantry-install-y-cover-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-051-s12-basegantry-install-y-cover-1.png)
- 将整合线缆穿过三角空隙。  
  ![a2l-pack-053-s12-basegantry-route-harness-through-triangle.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-053-s12-basegantry-route-harness-through-triangle.png)
- 拿住整合线缆，倾斜取出打印机底座。  
  ![a2l-pack-054-s12-basegantry-tilt-remove-base.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-054-s12-basegantry-tilt-remove-base.png)

### 13. 解锁热床

- 移除高亮处的 4 颗热床安装螺丝。  
  ![a2l-pack-055-s13-heatbed-remove-4-screws.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-055-s13-heatbed-remove-4-screws.png)

### 14. 安装底座泡棉

- 将 Y 滑车推至最后端（远离屏幕的一侧）。  
  ![a2l-pack-056-s14-push-y-carriage-away.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-056-s14-push-y-carriage-away.png)

> 若未将 Y 滑车推至最后端即进行安装，将导致热床损坏。

- 抬起热床，安装热床后方的泡棉。  
  ![a2l-pack-056-s14-bedfoam-install-rear-foam.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-056-s14-bedfoam-install-rear-foam.png)
- 将安装了泡棉的热床，滑入 Y 滑车。  
  ![a2l-pack-057-s14-bedfoam-slide-heatbed-into-y-carriage-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-057-s14-bedfoam-slide-heatbed-into-y-carriage-1.png)
- 将热床线缆穿过泡棉。  
  ![a2l-pack-059-s14-bedfoam-route-heatbed-cable-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-059-s14-bedfoam-route-heatbed-cable-1.png)
- 抬起底座，放回底座后方泡棉。  
  ![a2l-pack-061-s14-bedfoam-place-rear-base-foam-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-061-s14-bedfoam-place-rear-base-foam-1.png)
- 向内轻拉热床线缆，使其外部与泡棉平齐。  
  ![a2l-pack-063-s14-bedfoam-align-heatbed-cable-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-063-s14-bedfoam-align-heatbed-cable-1.png)
- 用胶带固定整合线缆。  
  ![a2l-pack-069-s14-bedfoam-tape-harness.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-069-s14-bedfoam-tape-harness.png)
- 抬起底座，放回底座前泡棉。  
  ![a2l-pack-065-s14-bedfoam-place-front-base-foam.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-065-s14-bedfoam-place-front-base-foam.png)

> 请按照下图，用纸包住热床侧面需要安装泡棉的位置，以便在安装左右两侧泡棉时保护软磁铁。  
> ![a2l-pack-065-s14-use-paper-1.jpg](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-065-s14-use-paper-1.jpg)  
> ![a2l-pack-065-s14-use-paper-2.jpg](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-065-s14-use-paper-2.jpg)

- 安装热床左侧泡棉，并将其对齐空缺处。  
  ![a2l-pack-066-s14-bedfoam-install-left-side-foam-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-066-s14-bedfoam-install-left-side-foam-1.png)
- 安装热床右侧泡棉，并将其对齐空缺处。

> 靠近屏幕一侧的泡棉在靠近热床的位置有凸起部分，请注意正确安装。

![a2l-pack-068-s14-bedfoam-install-right-side-foam.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-068-s14-bedfoam-install-right-side-foam.png)

- 在图示位置，用扎带固定左右泡棉。并剪去扎带多余部分。  
  ![a2l-pack-070-s14-bedfoam-zip-tie-side-foams-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-070-s14-bedfoam-zip-tie-side-foams-1.png)

### 15. 放回配件

  ![a2l-pack-072-s15-accessories-place-ptfe-tubes-v2.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-072-s15-accessories-place-ptfe-tubes-v2.png)
- 放入打印机底座。  
  ![a2l-pack-073-s15-accessories-place-printer-base.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-073-s15-accessories-place-printer-base.png)
- 放回两侧纸卡。  
  ![a2l-pack-074-s15-accessories-place-side-paper-cards.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-074-s15-accessories-place-side-paper-cards.png)

  ![a2l-pack-075-s15-accessories-place-gantry-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-075-s15-accessories-place-gantry-1.png)

  ![a2l-pack-077-s15-accessories-tuck-toolhead-cable.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-077-s15-accessories-tuck-toolhead-cable.png)
- 如图，将配件盒、吐料组件、电源线、料盘支架放入对应的泡棉保护槽内。并用胶带固定吐料组件。  
  ![a2l-pack-078-s15-accessories-place-box-cutter-power-cord-spool-holder.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-078-s15-accessories-place-box-cutter-power-cord-spool-holder.png)  
  ![a2l-pack-080-s15-accessories-all-in-foam-v2.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-080-s15-accessories-all-in-foam-v2.png)
- 安装 AMS lite 主体两侧泡棉。  
  ![a2l-pack-081-s15-accessories-install-ams-lite-side-foam-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-081-s15-accessories-install-ams-lite-side-foam-1.png)
- 放入 AMS lite 主体。  
  ![a2l-pack-083-s15-accessories-place-ams-lite-body-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-083-s15-accessories-place-ams-lite-body-1.png)
- 放入上层泡棉，将料盘转轴放入对应的泡棉保护槽内。  
  ![a2l-pack-085-s15-accessories-place-upper-foam-and-axles-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-085-s15-accessories-place-upper-foam-and-axles-1.png)
- 放入 AMS lite 支架。  
  ![a2l-pack-087-s15-accessories-place-ams-lite-base.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-087-s15-accessories-place-ams-lite-base.png)
- 放回打印板和快速入门指南。  
  ![a2l-pack-088-s15-accessories-place-print-plate-quick-start-guide.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-088-s15-accessories-place-print-plate-quick-start-guide.png)

### 16. 封住包装

- 收起防潮袋。  
  ![a2l-pack-089-s16-seal-fold-moisture-bag-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-089-s16-seal-fold-moisture-bag-1.png)
- 用胶带封住防潮袋。  
  ![a2l-pack-091-s16-seal-tape-moisture-bag.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-091-s16-seal-tape-moisture-bag.png)
- 用胶带封住外箱。  
  ![a2l-pack-092-s16-seal-tape-outer-box-1.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/a2l/manual/packing/a2l-pack-092-s16-seal-tape-outer-box-1.png)
