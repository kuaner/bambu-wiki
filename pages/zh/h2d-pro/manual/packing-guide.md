## 操作步骤

### 关闭电源

- 在打印机底部放置三块泡棉；

![3_foam_pads.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/3_foam_pads.png)

- 下降热床；

![image_-_2025-08-08t095416.090.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t095416.090.png)

- 关闭电源；

![image_-_2025-08-08t095446.498.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t095446.498.png)

- 拔下电源线；

![image_-_2025-08-08t095450.285.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t095450.285.png)

- 抬起打印机一侧，取出急停按键；

![image_-_2025-08-08t095549.091.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t095549.091.png)

- 断开急停按键线缆；

![image_-_2025-08-08t095552.323.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t095552.323.png)

- 在背板上粘上安全钥匙；

![image_-_2025-08-08t095756.811.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t095756.811.png)

- 粘回自动排气格栅贴纸。

![exhaust_sticker.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/exhaust_sticker.png)  
![exhaust_sticker2.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/exhaust_sticker2.png)

### 断开 AMS 连接

- 按压气动接头，取下两根料管；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100517.119.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100517.119.png)  
![image_-_2025-08-08t100518.801.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100518.801.png)

- 按压 AMS HT 背部释放按钮，取出料管；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100548.168.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100548.168.png)

- 按压 AMS 2 Pro 背部释放按钮，取出料管；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100550.780.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100550.780.png)

- 按住插头上的卡扣，断开两根 6pin 线；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100553.993.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100553.993.png)

- 将泡棉重新放入 AMS 2 Pro 中；

![ams_2_pro_foam_pad.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/ams_2_pro_foam_pad.png)

### 固定工具头和拖链

- 重新扣回 Z 轴泡棉，并上推至顶部；

![lead_screw_pad.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/lead_screw_pad.png)  
![top_pad.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/top_pad.png)

- 安装工具头纸板；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100625.204.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100625.204.png)

- 放回空气滤芯上方的泡棉；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100632.076.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100632.076.png)

- 将工具头后移；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100637.602.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100637.602.png)

- 放置工具头拖链固定泡棉；

![chain_foam.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/chain_foam.png)

- 将拖链卡进泡棉槽中；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100641.476.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100641.476.png)

- 用扎带固定拖链和料管；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100652.359.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100652.359.png)

- 将扎带移至中间位置。

![image_-_2025-08-08t100655.972.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100655.972.png)

### 固定 X 轴

- 将扎带穿过框架上的两个洞孔；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100658.963.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100658.963.png)

- 绕过 X 轴穿出并拉紧，注意避让皮带！

![image_-_2025-08-08t100706.639.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100706.639.png)

- 将工具头往左推动，靠紧扎带；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100710.988.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100710.988.png)

- 另一侧同样将扎带穿过洞孔，绕过 X 轴穿出，注意避让皮带！

![image_-_2025-08-08t100714.810.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100714.810.png)

![image_-_2025-08-08t100718.615.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100718.615.png)

![image_-_2025-08-08t100730.937.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100730.937.png)

### 固定热床和 AMS

- 放置两侧固定泡棉；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100557.269.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100557.269.png)  
![image_-_2025-08-08t100600.684.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100600.684.png)

- 在所示位置贴上贴纸，防止后续拧螺丝时刮蹭。

![image_-_2025-08-08t100603.684.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100603.684.png)

- 放回热床固定件，拧紧 2 颗螺丝；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100607.287.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100607.287.png)  
![image_-_2025-08-08t100610.822.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100610.822.png)

- 拧紧热床左右两侧固定螺丝。

![left_heatbed.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/right_heatbed.png)  
![left_heatbed.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/left_heatbed.png)

- 对准螺丝孔位，放回 AMS 底部固定件；

![fixture.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/fixture.png)

- 拧紧 4 颗螺丝；

![4_screws.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/4_screws.png)

- 放置配件盒底部泡棉纸板；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100738.726.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100738.726.png)

- 放置 AMS 保护泡棉；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100742.354.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100742.354.png)

- 将 AMS 2 Pro 如图示方向插紧固定件中；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100749.943.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100749.943.png)

- 锁紧固定件螺丝；

![bottom_fixture_2_screws.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/bottom_fixture_2_screws.png)  
![bottom_fixture_2_screws-2.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/bottom_fixture_2_screws-2.png)

- 安装 AMS 2 Pro 左固定件，并卡到位；

![left_fixing_part.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/left_fixing_part.png)

- 拧紧固定件螺丝；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100848.662.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100848.662.png)

- 安装右侧固定件；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100905.989.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100905.989.png)

- 拧紧固定螺丝；

![image_-_2025-08-08t100901.236.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100901.236.png)

- 拧紧正面的两颗固定螺丝；

![image_-_2025-08-08t101114.278.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101114.278.png)

![image_-_2025-08-08t100910.066.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t100910.066.png)

- 在 AMS 2 Pro 顶部放置泡棉；

![image_-_2025-08-08t101121.180.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101121.180.png)

- 在 AMS 2 Pro 上方左右两侧放置泡棉纸板；

![image_-_2025-08-08t101124.371.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101124.371.png)  
![image_-_2025-08-08t101130.816.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101130.816.png)

- 按图示位置放置 AMS HT，注意出料口需放在右侧泡棉空隙内；

![image_-_2025-08-08t101132.854.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101132.854.png)

### 放置配件盒和上盖玻璃

- 将配件装回配件盒；

![acc_box.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/acc_box.png)

- 在线缆配件盒上套上泡棉；

![image_-_2025-08-08t101117.185.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101117.185.png)

- 放置配件盒；

![image_-_2025-08-08t101137.134.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101137.134.png)

- 放置顶部三块泡棉；

![image_-_2025-08-08t101149.775.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101149.775.png)

- 装回上盖玻璃纸板；

![top_plate_cardboard.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/top_plate_cardboard.png)

- 放置上盖玻璃，并粘紧两侧胶带；

![image_-_2025-08-08t101152.820.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101152.820.png)

- 套上前门玻璃保护袋，粘紧前门；

![image_-_2025-08-08t101201.049.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101201.049.png)

![image_-_2025-08-08t101204.184.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101204.184.png)

### 装箱

- 将防潮袋套在纸箱底部的 4 个角，两人合力抬入打印机；

![two_people.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/two_people.png)

- 将缓冲气垫放至打印机四周；

![proof.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/proof.png)

- 放回纸板，确保放置到底；

![cardboard.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/cardboard.png)

- 将配件装进配件盒内；

![image_-_2025-08-08t101207.780.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101207.780.png)

![image_-_2025-08-08t101216.857.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/image_-_2025-08-08t101216.857.png)

- 放回顶部泡棉和配件盒；

![top_foam_pad.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/top_foam_pad.png)

![top_acc_box.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/top_acc_box.png)

- 套入外箱，确保底部 4 个孔对齐。

![4_holes.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/4_holes.png)

- 装入底部 4 个卡扣，并锁紧。

![clicks.png](https://public-cdn.bblmw.com/wiki/new/h2/h2d-pro/manual/packing-guide/clicks.png)
