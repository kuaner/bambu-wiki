## 视频指南

## 重要提醒

> 请务必按照步骤固定挂架和 AMS 2 Pro，避免在运输中与其他部件发生碰撞，导致外观或结构受损，影响您的退换货权益。

## 操作步骤

### 放回热床底部泡棉和辅助部件冷却风扇下面的泡棉

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/2.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/52.png)

### 将热床降至底部，压紧泡棉

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/1.png)

### 关机并拔出电源线。

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/3.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/4.png)

### 拔出安全钥匙，放回包装袋后贴至打印机背部

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/5.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/6.png)

### 拧紧气泵固定螺丝（适用于激光套装）

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/7.png)

### 按压移除料盘支架的铁氟龙料管

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/8.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/9.png)

### 向上推动移除料盘支架

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/10.png)

### 移除 6-pin 线

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/11.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/12.png)

### 按压移除铁氟龙料管

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/13.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/14.png)

### 放回 AMS 2 Pro 进料口上方的泡棉

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/15.png)

### 将配件放回至 AMS 2 Pro 中，关闭上盖并锁紧卡扣

配件包括：

- 排烟管、卡箍、排烟管转接件、气管（仅适用于激光套装）
- 铁氟龙料管、打印机电源线

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/16.png)

### 将挂架的两排调整至如图所示位置，并放回泡棉

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/17.png)

### 如图所示，放回挂架两侧的泡棉后，用扎带固定

> 请务必用扎带固定挂架，避免挂架在运输中与其他部件碰撞导致变形，影响您的退换货权益。

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/18.png)

### 放回空气滤芯上方的泡棉

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/19.png)

### 装回工具头上的卡纸

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/20.png)

### 先将泡棉放至拖链右方槽位内，一边缓慢向打印机后部拉动工具头，一边将拖链收纳进泡棉内，直到线轨压紧空气滤芯上方的泡棉

> 请勿掰动拖链，避免将其损坏。

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/21.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/22.png)

### 如图所示，在工具头两侧以及拖链用扎带固定

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/53.png)

### 如图所示，在三根丝杆顶部放回固定泡棉

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/23.png)

### 放回热床上的固定件后，拧紧四颗螺丝固定热床

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/24.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/25.png)

### 放回 AMS 2 Pro 的运输固定件后，拧紧四颗螺丝将其固定

> 请务必按照下方步骤固定 AMS 2 Pro，避免其在运输中与其他部件碰撞导致外观受损，影响您的退换货权益。

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/26.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/27.png)

### 放回泡棉后，将 AMS 2 Pro 放在运输固定件上

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/28.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/29.png)

### 放回 AMS 2 Pro 的两个顶部固定件，然后拧紧图示螺丝将 AMS 2 Pro 固定

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/30.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/31.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/32.png)

### 先后将两块泡棉放至打印机和 AMS 2 Pro 中间的区域，泡棉的凹面紧贴 AMS 2 Pro

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/33.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/34.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/35.png)

### 放回长方形配件盒（适用于激光套装）

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/36.png)

### 放回泡棉

> 长方形配件盒上的泡棉适用于激光套装。

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/37.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/38.png)

### 将上盖放入卡纸后，放至打印机顶部，并用贴纸固定

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/39.png)

### 两人合力将打印机抬入底部纸箱

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/40.png)

### 上拉防潮袋套，完全套入打印机后，用胶带封好上方开口。

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/41.png)

### 放回气垫、插回纸板和泡沫包装，确保放置到位。

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/42.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/43.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/44.png)

### 放回顶部的泡棉、耗材盒（激光套装）/配件盒（AMS 套装）和快速入门指南

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/45.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/46.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/47.png)

### 套入外箱，确保底部四个孔位对齐

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/48.png)

### 装入底部 4 个卡扣，并锁紧。

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/49.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/50.png)

![](https://wiki.bambulab.com/h2c/manual/packing/51.png)
